Rescuable Stagnation: A Diagnostic for Benchmark Suitability in LLM-Guided Materials Optimization

Bayesian Optimization & Active Learning digest 8.00 selection n/a

This paper proposes a diagnostic criterion, 'rescuable stagnation', to evaluate the suitability of benchmarks for materials optimization using LLM-guided methods. The approach is demonstrated on a benchmark derived from Materials Project data, revealing no statistically significant improvement by LLM-guided rescue over heuristic injection. This criterion allows for testing benchmark suitability before experiments commence, ensuring more reliable conclusions about the effectiveness of LLM-guided discovery.

Temperature-induced Changes to the Nucleation Mechanism of MOF-5-inspired Crystals through the Lens of Multiscale Simulation

Computational Chemistry digest 4.00 selection n/a

This study employs molecular simulation to investigate the impact of temperature on the nucleation mechanism of MOF-5-inspired crystals. A multiscale approach, including coarse-grained molecular dynamics simulations enhanced with Hamiltonian replica exchange, is used to construct free energy surfaces and minimum free energy pathway analysis. The results suggest that the temperature of 413 K, as typically used in MOF-5's solvothermal synthesis in DMF, is more kinetically favorable compared to both 300 K and 500 K, offering valuable insights for optimizing crystal growth processes.

Development of the 12-6-4-3B Model: Incorporating Nonadditive Three-Body Interactions into Metal Ion Simulations

Computational Chemistry digest 4.00 selection n/a

The 12-6-4-3B model, a refined classical force field, is proposed based on Extended Discrete Averaging Potentials (EDA) and physical reasoning. This model introduces three-body interactions, significantly improving its accuracy in simulating [Zn(H2O)n]2+ clusters (n = 2 - 6), particularly in reproducing the water exchange mechanism and associated free energy barrier. This enhancement underscores the practical benefit of considering nonadditive three-body effects in metal ion simulations.

Structure-Based Modeling & Docking digest 8.00 selection n/a

This paper presents an integrated fragment-based drug discovery (FBDD) approach that combines pharmacophore modeling, structure-based virtual screening, molecular dynamics simulations, and extensive biological validation to identify novel MMP-9 PEX domain inhibitors. The study focuses on matrix metalloproteinase-9 (MMP-9), a key mediator of extracellular matrix remodeling and cancer metastasis, for which the development of selective inhibitors has been challenging due to high conservation of its catalytic domain. The work highlights the discovery of potential novel MMP-9 PEX domain inhibitors, offering a promising chemical scaffold for further preclinical development, although the specific efficacy and selectivity of these compounds remain to be determined in more detailed studies.
